# Expense policy (sample)

## Meals
Team meals are reimbursable up to $60 per person when at least three people attend and the purpose is recorded. Solo meals while travelling are capped at $75 per day.

## Travel
Book economy for flights under six hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights over six hours with manager approval. Hotels are capped at $250 per night in tier-1 cities and $180 elsewhere.

## Software
Software subscriptions under $50 per month can be expensed without approval if they are used for work. Anything above that needs a ticket to IT so the licence can be centrally managed.

## Submitting
Submit receipts within 30 days. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed. Every claim needs an itemised receipt, not just a card statement.
