# New starter handbook (sample)

## First week
On day one you get your laptop from IT and access to email, chat and the code host. Your manager schedules a 30/60/90-day plan conversation in the first week. Pair with your onboarding buddy for at least two sessions.

## Working hours
Core collaboration hours are 10:00–15:00 local time. Outside those hours, asynchronous communication is the default; nobody is expected to reply after hours.

## Time off
Everyone gets 25 days of paid leave plus public holidays. Book leave in the HR system at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days.

## Security
Use the password manager for every work credential. Enable two-factor authentication on all accounts on day one. Report lost devices to security within one hour.
