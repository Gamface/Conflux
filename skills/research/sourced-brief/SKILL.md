# Sourced brief

1. **Scope** — restate the question in one line and list 3-6 sub-questions in the `todo` plan.
2. **Gather** — for each sub-question:
   - search the knowledge base first (`knowledge_search`), then the web (`web_search` → `web_fetch`, or the browser for pages that need JavaScript or a login);
   - record the URL or document title next to every fact you keep.
3. **Cross-check** — any number, date or claim that matters needs two independent sources, or is marked *single-source*.
4. **Write** the brief:
   - **Answer** (2-3 sentences, the bottom line first)
   - **Findings** — bullets, each ending with its source in brackets
   - **What I could not find** — be explicit; never paper over gaps
   - **Sources** — numbered list
5. **Save** it with `page_save` (title: `Brief — <topic>`), and reply with the bottom line plus the page link.

## Pitfalls
- Search-result snippets are not sources; open the page.
- Prefer primary sources (official docs, filings, papers) over aggregators.
- Do not quote more than a sentence from any single source — paraphrase.
